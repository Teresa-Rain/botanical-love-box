You were different

The realization that you were different did not hit me all at once. And knowing me, if it had, I wouldn't have trusted it. I noticed it slowly...in the way you listened to me so closely. The way you responded so intentionally to even my most mundane thoughts. The way you seemed to really see ME...not just the face I wore for most people. But the moment that made me really see it was a moment you had crossed one of my boundaries. I told you so fully expecting everything to end in that moment or at least to slowly taper off after. But you surprised me..you apologized, you took responsibility, and changed the way you approached me. Immediately. No defensiveness. No anger. No trying to convince me I was wrong or overthinking it or that you were just joking. You gave me humility, care, and change. I didn't realize how much I needed those things until you gave them to me freely, Dipu.. You didn't feel like any man I'd ever interacted with before. Soon after, I was letting myself get prepared to fall for you.
